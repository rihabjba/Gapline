Method

What Gapline measures

Gapline compares each market's AI adoption in 2025 with the level its 2023 cloud adoption implies on a line fitted across all 27 markets. The difference is the conversion gap: a large negative gap means capability exists and is not being converted into use.

The same published adoption figure can hide two situations. Gapline separates a market that lacks digital foundations from one that has them and does not use them.

How a market is classified

1
Gap of −5 pp or lower
Stalled foundations
2
Gap of +5 pp or higher
Converting well
3
Cloud more than 6 pp below the EU mean
Foundations missing
4
Everything else
On trend

Rules apply in this order. The ±5 pp boundary is a stated judgement, not a value derived from the data.

How a prediction is calculated

An ordinary least squares line is fitted across the 27 markets: predicted AI 2025 = 2.913 + 0.3806 × cloud 2023. The slope is 0.381: ten points more cloud go with about 3.8 points more AI. The line explains 37% of the variation (R² = 0.373).

The conversion gap is observed minus predicted. Italy: 2.913 + 0.3806 × 61.4 = 26.3% predicted, 16.4% observed, gap −9.9 pp.

Confidence rule for stalled markets

The gap is also computed two more ways: industry-adjusted (average residual from the model with 50 sector fixed effects) and net of prior adoption (residual from the model that adds 2021 AI adoption).

A stalled market is confirmed if both adjusted gaps are also −5 pp or lower, and provisional otherwise. On 2025 data: Poland and Malta confirmed; Cyprus, Italy, Hungary, Ireland and Portugal provisional.

What it does not claim

  • It shows sequence, not cause. There is no counterfactual.
  • The gap is inferred from a remainder, not measured directly.
  • With 27 markets, null results carry little weight. Skills are reported as not detected, not ruled out.
  • Findings are about markets, not firms. Firms under 10 employees are out of scope.

Data

Eurostat, Community Survey on ICT Usage and E-Commerce in Enterprises (isoc_eb_ai, isoc_eb_ain2, isoc_cicce_use, isoc_ske_itspe, isoc_ske_itts) and national accounts (nama_10_pc). EU-27 figures are simple averages of the 27 markets (AI adoption 2025: 20.7%; Eurostat's weighted EU figure is 20.0%).
